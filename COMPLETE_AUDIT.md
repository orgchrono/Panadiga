## 5. Physical Safety Invariants & Quality Verification

2. **Two-Layer NOR Write Protection (`IC5001` Spansion `S29JL064H`):** Layer 1 IPL (`256 KiB`: `0x80000000..=0x8003FFFF` and `0x84000000..=0x8403FFFF`, labeled `SA0..SA3` in abstract `64 KiB` Pico policy units / physically `SA0..SA10` = `8×8 KiB + 3×64 KiB` on silicon) is **permanently read-only (`immutable RO`, never writable → `E05`)** on the Pico probe; Layer 2 BGO/App Upper NOR (`0x80040000..=0x807EFFFF` and `0x84040000..=0x847EFFFF`, `SA4..SA133` in policy / physically `SA11..SA133`) remains write-protected by default (`NvramOnly` → `E05`), unlockable only via explicit `CONFIRM_UPPER_NOR` opt-in / CoW `panadiga nvram patch-upper-nor` (and guarded by RAG `HardwareSafetyValidator`).
3. **Ghidra Database Integrity:** `C:\ghidra\projects\Panadiga_MN103S` is never modified by tests, CLI runs, or CI checks.
